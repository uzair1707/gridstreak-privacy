Frequently Asked Questions

What is GridStreak?

GridStreak is a habit tracking app that turns your daily habits into visual patterns. Every time you log a habit, a block fills in — showing your progress as a growing pattern. Stay consistent and watch it come to life.

How do I get started?

Open the app, create up to 3 habits, choose a pattern, and start logging. That's it. You can sign up to save your progress, or try it first before creating an account.

How do streaks work?

A streak counts consecutive days where you've logged at least one habit. Miss a day and the streak resets. The longer your streak, the more your flame icon evolves — from fire to ice to gold to cosmic.

What are badges?

Badges unlock automatically as you hit milestones — like logging your first habit, completing a pattern, or hitting a 7-day streak. View them all in the Achievements tab.

Can I change my habits?

You can create up to 3 habits. Habit editing and deletion is coming in a future update.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

I'm not receiving notifications. What should I do?

Check that notifications are enabled for GridStreak in your phone's Settings app. On some Android devices (Xiaomi, Samsung), you may also need to enable autostart for the app to ensure notifications are delivered reliably.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email us at support@gridstreak.app with a description of what happened and what device you're using. We'll look into it as quickly as possible.
